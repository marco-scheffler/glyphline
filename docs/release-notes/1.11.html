Your API credit, next to your quota

If your Anthropic login also has a Claude Console organisation with prepaid API credit, Glyphline can now show it. Under Accounts, the new link button on a Claude account signs in to the Console once, in the same private session as the account itself. From then on the account's card shows how much credit is left, how much lapses next and when, and what this month has cost so far.

The menu bar panel shows the balance on the account's own line, so the panel does not get taller. When credit is about to lapse within a week, an hourglass with the remaining time appears beside it.

Promotional credit, such as the monthly API credit that comes with a subscription, expires unused. Glyphline now tells you three days before that happens, and again when your balance drops below 5 dollars. Both follow the "Warn before quota or credit runs out" setting.

Glyphline only reads your balance; it never buys credit or changes anything in the Console. Disconnecting the Console leaves the account's claude.ai sign-in untouched.

Requires macOS 26.
