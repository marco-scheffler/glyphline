The Fable allowance has a row of its own

Claude now reports a separate weekly allowance for Fable, next to the weekly limit on everything else. Glyphline did not read it, so the menu could show plenty of weekly quota left while Fable was already used up. It now has its own row in the menu bar panel and its own card on the dashboard, with the same reset countdown, pace and running-out warning as the other windows.

A spent Fable allowance turns its own row red, but not the menu bar status: every other model still works.

Windows that no longer exist disappear

Verified Ultra accounts no longer have a five-hour limit. When Claude stops reporting a window, Glyphline stops showing it after the next successful update, instead of keeping the last figure on screen marked "as of …". Your recorded history is kept.

Requires macOS 26.
